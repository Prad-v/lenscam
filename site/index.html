Lenscam

Shoot once. Keep the master. Read it on the iPhone.

A capture is the file the camera wrote, plus a sidecar. Translation, captions, and briefs sit beside that file. They are not written into it.

Take a picture

  1. Tap the sliders icon for the looks, including Grain, and for Exposure through Mono. Import filter reads a JSON file of saturation, contrast, temperature, and grain. Use the bolt for flash and the rotating arrows to switch cameras. The moon is night mode in every genre. The dial is manual ISO, shutter, white balance, and focus. The aperture icon is Camera Assistant: quick tap, palm, voice, a floating shutter, lens switching, and focus tuning. Then swipe the names above the shutter until the genre you want sits on the gold mark: Photography, Portrait, Landscape, Nature, Food, Wildlife, Sport, or Cinematic.
  2. Read the zoom pill. It says Optical, Sensor crop, Digital, Past sharp crop, or Not measured.
  3. The genre sets the lens, focus, and exposure. If the frame needs one more change, tap Apply, then press the shutter.
  4. Tap the sliders icon for filters and the exposure tools. Enhance copy, when it is on, is a separate file. The dial icon opens manual ISO, shutter, focus, white balance, RAW when the phone supports it, presets, and the histogram, zebra, and focus-peaking guides.
  5. Tap the shutter for one photo. Hold it for a burst in any still genre. The sharpest frame is the master, and the other frames stay labeled as burst files. Sport takes a four-frame burst on a tap. Cine records until you press again.
  6. Open the library for the hash, the master, and any labeled copy.

A coach line shows up only when the shot is not ready. Read, Brief, and Evidence live in the more menu. On a simulator with no camera, use Import. That still is marked “Not measured” because no lens was involved.

Genres

GenreWhat you leave with
PhotographyThe master only. ProRAW when the phone supports it.
PortraitLive depth blur, lighting, and an f-stop. The master keeps the original and the depth map. The portrait copy is separate.
LandscapeThe master, plus a copy that can straighten a measured horizon.
NatureThe master, plus a copy with a little edge contrast. Detail is not invented.
FoodThe wide lens, the Warm look when you have not chosen another, and a warmer food.jpg beside the master.
WildlifeThe longest optical lens. The copy does not reconstruct fur or feathers.
SportA tap takes four frames. Hold the shutter to keep going. The sharpest frame is the master. The other frames stay labeled as burst files.
CinematicAn ungraded recording. A letterboxed still is a separate file.

Profiles

ProfileWhat you leave with
MasterThe largest still. ProRAW when the phone supports it, with a preview beside the hashed raw file.
ClarityThe master, plus clarity.jpg. The copy is sharpened. The master is not.
ZoomAn honest meter. Lenscam does not invent detail past the sharp crop.
ReadText and barcodes from the still, then a translation when that language is installed.
InterviewThe video, the original audio, a transcript, captions.srt, and a brief written from the words.
BriefA title, summary, and next steps from the text in the frame. Empty text does not become a guessed description.
EvidenceA hashed master, optional location, and the text that was read. No brief. No sharpened copy.

Translate

In the library, tap the speech bubble and choose the language. If the pack is not on the iPhone yet, open the capture and tap Translate. The system download keeps the original wording in the sidecar.

Trust the file

Every master has a SHA-256. Copy it from the capture. Evidence can share a one-page report with that hash, the zoom band, the location if you allowed it, and the text that was read. Saving to Photos makes a copy. The hashed file stays in Lenscam.

Ask Siri

  • “Start an evidence shoot in Lenscam.”
  • “Read text with Lenscam.”

Where the system includes Visual Intelligence for apps, a look through the system camera can offer “Read in Lenscam.”

When something is missing

No brief, and the note says nothing readable
The model only writes from text Vision or the transcript already found. Fill the frame with the page, or check the microphone.
Apple Intelligence is off
Capture, zoom, reading, and translation continue. Briefs wait until the model is on and ready.
No location on an evidence shot
Permission was off, or the phone had no fix yet. The hash is still there.
